# Overview

The Coca-Cola Company owns or licenses beverage brands sold in more than 200 countries and territories. Its portfolio includes Coca-Cola, Sprite, Fanta, Diet Coke/Coca-Cola Light, water, sports drinks, coffee, tea, juice, dairy and plant-based beverages. A significant part of the business sells concentrates and syrups to independent bottling partners. Sources: 2025 Form 10-K, lines 143–144 and 545, accession 0001628280-26-010047.

Fiscal 2025 net operating revenues were **$47,941 million**, compared with **$47,061 million** in 2024. Revenue growth was **1.87%**, calculated as `(47,941 - 47,061) / 47,061`. The filing presents this line as net operating revenues; this report uses that definition consistently. Source: 2025 Form 10-K income statement, accession 0001628280-26-010047; XBRL tag `Revenues`, period 2025-12-31.

Operating income was **$13,762 million**. Operating margin was **28.71%**, calculated as `13,762 / 47,941`. Net income attributable to Coca-Cola shareowners was **$13,107 million**, and GAAP diluted EPS was **$3.04**. Source: 2025 Form 10-K income statement, accession 0001628280-26-010047; tags `OperatingIncomeLoss`, `NetIncomeLossAvailableToCommonStockholdersBasic`, and `EarningsPerShareDiluted`, period 2025-12-31.

# Market Positioning and Growth Outlook

The filing describes brand reach and the bottling network as the core competitive position. Coca-Cola also states that trademarks are important to its business and that independent bottlers distribute products in most markets. Sources: 2025 Form 10-K, lines 143–144, 325–326 and 550, accession 0001628280-26-010047.

The latest quarter supports a positive operating trend. Net operating revenues were **$13,380 million**, compared with **$12,455 million** in the comparable prior-year quarter. Growth was **7.43%**, calculated in code as `(13,380 - 12,455) / 12,455`. GAAP diluted EPS was **$0.93**, compared with $0.86. The release also states that global unit case volume grew 5% and that the company gained value share in total nonalcoholic ready-to-drink beverages. Source: Exhibit 99.1, lines 28–34 and 258–282, accession 0001628280-26-067403, quarter ended 2026-10-02.

The main catalyst is continued volume, concentrate-sales and margin execution. The release says quarterly net revenue grew 7%, concentrate sales grew 7%, and reported operating margin increased to 33.4% from 32.0%. A key risk is that the 10-K calls the commercial beverage industry highly competitive and says competition can restrict pricing and increase marketing costs. Sources: Exhibit 99.1, lines 28–30; 2025 Form 10-K, lines 423–427.
