# Worksheet contract

Required facts: parent; distribution date and ratio; dated price; market value; screen rank and points; after-tax earnings; depreciation; capital spending; transferred debt, liabilities, cash, and restrictions; parent facts; insider purchases; and median daily dollar volume.

For each fact, record the value, statement basis, filing, section, date, and source link. The statement basis is `historical`, `pro forma`, or `reported after spin`.

Owner cash flow is after-tax earnings plus depreciation minus capital spending. The student's file controls the low and mid valuation method and rates. The code prints the inputs and arithmetic.

Only the persona judges why the name may be cheap and what would change the view. The worksheet is a screen, not a recommendation.
